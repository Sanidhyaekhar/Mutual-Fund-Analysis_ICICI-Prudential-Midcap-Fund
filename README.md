# ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund — Mutual Fund Analysis Report

A Comprehensive analysis report on **ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund (Direct Plan) (Growth)**, built in Excel, covering return analysis, risk-adjusted performance, and portfolio composition — benchmarked against the Nifty Midcap 150 TRI, Nifty 50 TRI, and category averages.

## 📌 Objective

This project applies financial analysis and mutual fund research concepts to a real, actively managed mid cap equity scheme, with the goal of evaluating:
- How the fund has performed relative to its benchmark and category, across both lumpsum and SIP modes of investment
- Whether its returns have been backed by sound risk-adjusted metrics, not just raw performance
- What the underlying portfolio (sector tilts, valuation, concentration) reveals about the fund manager's investment style

## 📊 What's Covered

**Fund & Scheme Details -**
Inception date, AUM, plan type, benchmark, expense ratio, exit load, turnover ratio, and fund manager details.

**Portfolio Breakdown -**
Top 10 stock holdings, top 5 sector holdings, and asset allocation.

**Riskometer -**
Risk classification for both the scheme and its benchmark.

**Return Analysis -**
CAGR (lumpsum) and XIRR (SIP) returns across 1,3,5 and 7-year periods, benchmarked against the Nifty Midcap 150 TRI and Nifty 50 TRI, along with growth-of-investment tables.

**Risk Analysis -**
Standard deviation, Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, Beta, and Alpha — compared against the benchmark and category average.

**Portfolio Analysis -**
Valuation ratios (P/E, P/B, P/S, P/CF), portfolio concentration (number of stocks, Top 3, 5 and 10 weightage), average market capitalization, sector type exposure (Cyclical, Sensitive/], Defensive), market cap weightage (Large, Mid, Small), and detailed sector-wise exposure vs. category.

**Interpretation -**
Interpretation of Overall fund , it's Return, Risk and portfolio is written after ever section respectively.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

- Microsoft Excel — Data structuring, formulas, conditional formatting, native charts
- Financial analysis — CAGR, XIRR, Sharpe, Sortino, Alpha, Beta
- Mutual fund research conventions — Riskometer, AMFI disclosure standards
