# ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund — Mutual Fund Analysis Report

An independent analysis report on **ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund (Direct Plan)**, built in Excel, covering return analysis, risk-adjusted performance, and portfolio composition — benchmarked against the Nifty Midcap 150 TRI, Nifty 50 TRI, and category averages.

## 📌 Objective

This project applies financial analysis and mutual fund research concepts to a real, actively managed mid cap equity scheme, with the goal of evaluating:
- How the fund has performed relative to its benchmark and category, across both lumpsum and SIP modes of investment
- Whether its returns have been backed by sound risk-adjusted metrics, not just raw performance
- What the underlying portfolio (sector tilts, valuation, concentration) reveals about the fund manager's investment style

## 📊 What's Covered

**Fund & Scheme Details**
Inception date, AUM, plan type, benchmark, expense ratio, exit load, turnover ratio, and fund manager details.

**Portfolio Snapshot**
Top 10 stock holdings, top 5 sector holdings, and asset allocation (equity vs. cash).

**Riskometer**
SEBI-mandated risk classification for both the scheme and its benchmark.

**Return Analysis**
CAGR (lumpsum) and XIRR (SIP) returns across 1/3/5/7-year periods, benchmarked against the Nifty Midcap 150 TRI and Nifty 50 TRI, along with growth-of-investment tables.

**Risk Analysis**
Standard deviation, Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, Beta, and Alpha — compared against the benchmark and category average.

**Portfolio Analysis**
Valuation ratios (P/E, P/B, P/S, P/CF), portfolio concentration (number of stocks, Top 3/5/10 weightage), average market capitalization, sector type exposure (Cyclical/Sensitive/Defensive), market cap weightage (Large/Mid/Small), and detailed sector-wise exposure vs. category.

## 🧠 Key Observations

- The fund has outperformed both benchmarks across most periods and in both lumpsum and SIP modes, with the sole exception of the 7-year lumpsum CAGR, where it has modestly trailed the Nifty Midcap 150 TRI.
- Superior risk-adjusted metrics (Sharpe of 0.98, Sortino of 1.40) suggest the fund's returns haven't come at the cost of disproportionate risk.
- The portfolio trades at a valuation discount to its category (P/E of ~21x vs. ~26x), pointing to a value-oriented stock selection approach.
- Sector positioning is heavily tilted toward Basic Materials and Industrials, with a marked underweight to Technology and Healthcare relative to peers — reflecting a cyclical, manufacturing-linked portfolio.
